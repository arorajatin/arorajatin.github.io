Product Engineer · Former Founder · Bangalore

Hey, I'm Jatin. I'm a full-stack product engineer with 12+ years of building and shipping software, most of them spent owning products end to end.

Until April 2026 I was co-founder and CTO of Utilize, a no-code platform for building apps on top of Google Sheets and Notion. I built the app builder from scratch in React, Node.js and MongoDB, and worked on everything from UX and customer success to pricing, fundraising and the acquisition of our Notion product.

a bit about me

Before Utilize I co-founded StretchCloud, an edge-computing bet backed by Entrepreneur First. Before that I was a senior engineer at PhonePe, working on the first version of its iOS payments app and its in-app micro-app store, and at Hike Messenger, building messaging, auth and media handling for iOS.

I like high-agency, ambiguous environments where the job is to figure out what to build as much as how. Lately I've also been writing about life outside of work, starting with ten days of silence at a Vipassana course.

Have a look at my experience and projects, or grab my resume.
